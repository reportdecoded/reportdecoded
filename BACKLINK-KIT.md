## 2. SourceBottle / journalist pitch (earns high-authority editorial links)

Sign up at sourcebottle.com (free, AU). Watch the **Property & Real Estate** and
**Business & Finance** categories. When a journalist asks about building
inspections, buying costs, or property defects, reply with this:

> Hi [name], I'm Morgan, founder of Report Decoded — an Australian tool that
> decodes building & pest inspection reports for buyers. Happy to give a quick
> expert comment on [their topic]. A few things buyers consistently get wrong:
> [1–2 sharp, specific insights, e.g. "concrete cancer sounds catastrophic but is
> almost always a price-negotiation issue, not a deal-breaker — the cost gap
> between a cosmetic patch and structural repair is roughly $3k vs $80k"].
> Full name/title for attribution: Morgan [surname], Founder, Report Decoded
> (reportdecoded.com.au). Happy to expand on any of this.

Getting quoted in one property article = a genuine editorial backlink, the kind
that actually moves rankings.

## 4. Forums (value-first, NOT link-dropping)

Genuinely help, link only when it directly answers the question, and DISCLOSE you
built it. Anything else reads as spam and can backfire.

- **PropertyChat** (propertychat.com.au) — huge AU buyer community
- **Reddit:** r/AusPropertyChat, r/AusFinance, r/melbourne/sydney (property threads)

Value-first answer template (adapt to the actual thread):

> The report is basically flagging [plain-English explanation of their issue].
> For [defect], the cost usually lands around [range] depending on [factor], and
> it's normally a negotiation point rather than a dealbreaker. (Full disclosure —
> I built a tool called Report Decoded that does this kind of translation, but
> honestly even a specialist [trade] quote before you go unconditional will tell
> you what you need to know.)

Rule: give the answer first, mention the tool second, disclose always. Post on
threads where it genuinely helps — 2–3 good ones beat 20 drive-by links.

## Reality check

Backlinks work slowly — expect weeks-to-months, not days. 5–10 quality, relevant
links (editorial, directory, a guest post) will do more than 100 junk ones. Avoid
anyone selling "1000 backlinks for $20" — that's exactly what triggers penalties.
Pair this with the on-page cluster work already done and give it a couple of
months.
