## Batch Filming Tips

Film 4 reels in one session:
- Same location, plain background (kitchen bench or desk)
- Record each talking-head in one take, 60 seconds max
- Export carousels from Canva (same template each time for brand consistency)
- Caption every reel — 85% of TikTok/IG reels watched without sound
