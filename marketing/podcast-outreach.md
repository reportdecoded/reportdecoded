## What to Offer

**For podcast guests:**
- Free report for the host to try before recording
- Discount code for their listeners (e.g. ELEPHANTROOM for 15% off)
- No expectation of endorsement — just a useful tool

**For social creators:**
- $15/report affiliate commission (existing programme)
- $10 off discount code for their audience
- 3 free reports to try the product themselves

**What a good outcome looks like:**
- 1 podcast mention → 50–200 website visits, 5–20 sales
- 1 Instagram collab post (20K followers, 5% ER) → 1,000 reach, 10–30 visits, 2–5 sales
- Target: reach out to 3 podcasts + 5 creators = likely 1–2 conversions to collab
