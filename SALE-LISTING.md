## What it does
Upload an AS4349.1 building/pest inspection PDF → the AI returns:
- **Verdict** — Proceed / Negotiate / Walk Away, in plain English
- **Per-defect repair cost estimates** — 2026 AU trade rates, calibrated by
  defect severity, region, access multipliers, and same-trade bundling
- **Honest "get a specialist assessment" flags** for un-estimatable defects
- **5-year capex forecast**
- **Local tradie matching** — 32-trade taxonomy, measured at 100% accuracy
  against a hand-labelled ground-truth set
- **Ready-to-send negotiation letter** with a recommended $ figure
- **White-label mode for buyer's agents** — their branding on the report
