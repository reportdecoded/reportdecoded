| **C — Sales Agent** | Listing/sales agent | Pre-listing analysis: run report before listing, set vendor expectations, defuse buyer-side claims | Lower priority, often defensive about defects |

## Engagement protocol

- **TikTok / Instagram:** follow → like 2-3 posts → comment substantively on a specific video → wait 12-24h → DM
- **LinkedIn:** accept connection → DM within 24h (no comment-first warming needed — accept IS the warming)
- **Follow-up rule:** No reply at 72h = ONE polite bump. No reply at 7 days = mark No Reply, move on.
