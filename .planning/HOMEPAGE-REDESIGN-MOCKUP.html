Deferred — under 88% confidence threshold

  • Replace italic <em>decoded.</em> with amber colour — 85% confidence. Aesthetic call; preview included in mockup but didn't make the cut for ship. Easy to revisit.
  • Quantified social-proof badges (e.g. "25+ analyses, avg negotiation $42K") — would be high-impact but no live customer count yet per marketing context. Park until first 10 paying agents.
  • Hero illustration / product screenshot — adds load weight, no proven uplift over text-led hero. Skip.
  • Palette change — current palette is mature and on-brand. Do not touch.
  • Testimonials block — no real customers yet (per marketing context). Adding a fake one would be a red flag.
